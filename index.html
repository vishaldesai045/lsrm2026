About the Event

This National Conference aims to provide a dynamic platform for academicians, researchers, industry experts, policymakers, professionals, and students to deliberate on emerging GST reforms and their role in advancing Atmanirbhar Bharat and Viksit Bharat 2047.

Arranged in collaboration with Indian Council of Social Science Research (ICSSR), New Delhi. Knowledge partners include the Gujarat State Tax Office and District Industries Centre (DIC) of Govt. of Gujarat.

Excursion Trip

A complimentary heritage excursion is offered to registered participants on the evening of the first day.

Explore three extraordinary treasures of North Gujarat: the architectural grandeur of Modhera Sun Temple, Rani-ki-Vav, the UNESCO World Heritage Site featured on India's ₹100 currency note, and Patola House, where you can discover the exquisite craftsmanship of Patan Patola, the world-renowned double-ikat textile tradition.

The spectacular Rani-ki-Vav Laser Show adds a magical dimension to this journey of light, history and storytelling. More than a sightseeing tour, this experience offers a memorable opportunity to connect with India's timeless heritage, culture and creativity while meeting fellow academicians, researchers and professionals.
